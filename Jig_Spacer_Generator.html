Spacer properties

Round sections sit in a circular cradle and rectangular sections in a square pocket, each opened up by the fit clearance; the seat stays at the section underside so the centreline height holds. Mass assumes solid PLA at 1.24 g/cm³, so a print with partial infill will be lighter. Copy DXF puts the profile on the clipboard as DXF text (1:1, mm) to paste into a .dxf file and extrude in CAD.
