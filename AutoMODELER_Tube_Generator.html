Estimate only; Inventor builds the real part. The view assumes the tubes this member meets run horizontally in the plane of dx and dy, with plain ends cut parallel to them.

Columns match the Tube Generator sheet, so a block copied from Excel pastes straight in. Enter moves down, Tab moves right, Ctrl+D fills from the cell above. dx and dy accept arithmetic, fractions and feet-inches: =2*12+3.25, 3 25/64, 2'-3 1/4".
